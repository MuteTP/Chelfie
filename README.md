# CHELFIE

**Your own photobooth, right on your Android phone or tablet.**

Set up CHELFIE at a wedding, a birthday, a party or a pop-up, and your guests can run the booth themselves. They walk up, tap the screen, strike a pose, and walk away with a printed photo in their hands. You don't need to run anything.

CHELFIE works with your phone's own camera, or with a **Sony ZV-E10** connected over USB for sharp, full-quality photos.

---

## Download & install

CHELFIE isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install it straight from this page.

1. Go to the [**Releases**](../../releases) page and download **CHELFIE.apk** on your Android phone or tablet.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps**.
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

> Android may warn you that the app comes from an unknown source. This is normal for apps installed from outside the Play Store. Tap **Install anyway** (or **More details › Install anyway**) to continue.

**Requirements:** an Android phone or tablet. To use a Sony ZV-E10, the device needs a USB-C port that can connect to accessories (most modern phones and tablets can).

## How it works

Here's what a guest goes through, from tapping the screen to holding a print.

### 1. Tap to start
The booth waits on a welcome screen with your event's name on it. A guest taps anywhere to begin.

### 2. Choose a layout
Guests pick how their print will look:

- **Single:** one big photo. The booth takes 4 shots and the guest keeps their favorite.
- **Strips:** the classic photobooth strip. 4 photos in a column, printed twice on the same sheet so it can be cut into two strips to share.
- **Grid:** 4 photos arranged in a 2×2 grid.

For Strips and Grid, the booth takes 6 shots, so there's room for a blink or a bad angle.

### 3. Strike a pose
A countdown appears on screen before every shot, so everyone has time to get ready. The screen shows how many photos are left. On a Sony camera, the preview works like a mirror, which makes posing feel natural.

### 4. Pick the best ones
All the shots appear on screen. Guests tap the ones they like, in the order they want them printed. Changed your mind? Tap a photo again to swap it out.

### 5. Set the mood
Guests choose a filter and watch the photos change right away:

**Original · Mono · Noir · Sepia · Warm · Cool · Vivid · Fade**

### 6. Pick a frame
Next comes the look of the paper around the photos:

- **White:** clean and simple.
- **Black:** bold and dramatic.
- **Gallery:** thin outlines and elegant serif lettering, like a gallery print.
- **Film:** sprocket holes along the edges, like a roll of film.
- **Soft:** a light gray background with rounded photo corners.
- **Cream:** warm, vintage-toned paper.

Every frame shows your event's name, and the date if you turn it on.

### 7. Take it home
The print is ready. Guests can:

- **Print** it on the spot.
- **Share** it to Messenger, Instagram, email or any other app.
- **Save it** to the device's gallery (when the photos were taken with the phone camera).

After a short while, the booth goes back to the welcome screen by itself, ready for the next guest.

Guests can go back a step at any time, or start over.

## Works with any printer

CHELFIE prints through Android's built-in printing, so **it works with any printer your phone or tablet can print to**: Wi-Fi printers, photo printers, inkjets and lasers from Canon, Epson, HP, Brother and others.

Each print is a standard **4×6 in (4R)** photo. When the print screen opens, pick your printer and paper size. CHELFIE fits the photo to the paper you choose, and Android remembers your choice for the next guest.

> **Recommended:** the **Canon SELPHY CP1500** makes great booth prints. Install **Mopria Print Service** from the Play Store, connect the printer to the same Wi-Fi as your device, load postcard paper, and choose the **Postcard (100 × 148 mm)** paper size.

If your printer doesn't appear in the list, install your printer brand's print app from the Play Store, or the free **Mopria Print Service**.

## Setting up your booth

### Step 1: Create a staff password
The first time you open CHELFIE, it asks you to create a staff password. This keeps guests out of the settings, so only you can change how the booth works.

### Step 2: Choose your camera
You have two options:

- **Phone camera:** nothing to set up. Mount the phone or tablet facing your guests and you're ready.
- **Sony ZV-E10:** for professional-quality photos. Connect the camera to your device with a USB-C cable and set the camera to PC Remote mode. CHELFIE walks you through it step by step under **Settings › ZV-E10 setup guide**. Once you've set it up, CHELFIE opens by itself whenever you plug in the camera.

When the Sony is plugged in, CHELFIE uses it. When it isn't, CHELFIE switches to the phone camera, so the booth keeps working.

### Step 3: Make it yours
In the settings, type your **event name** ("Anna & Mark's Wedding", "Office Party 2026"). It's printed on every photo. You can also choose whether to print the date.

### Step 4: Do a test print
In the settings, tap **Test print** to check that your printer is connected and the colors look right before guests arrive.

That's it. Your booth is ready.

## Settings

Your guests won't see the settings. To open them, **press and hold the `•••` button** in the top corner of the welcome screen, then enter your staff password.

There you can:

- **Change the camera:** use Auto, the Sony camera only, or the phone camera.
- **Set the countdown:** 2 to 10 seconds before each photo.
- **Set the reset timer:** how long the booth waits before returning to the welcome screen (15 to 120 seconds).
- **Edit your event name**, and turn the date on or off.
- **Turn the mirrored preview on or off** for the Sony camera.
- **Change your staff password.**
- **Run a test print.**
- **See the camera log** if the camera isn't connecting.

## Handy tips

- **Running a long event with the Sony?** The camera runs on its battery while it's connected. A dummy-battery adapter (NP-FW50 coupler) plugged into the wall keeps it going all day.
- **Camera not connecting?** Make sure the USB-C cable can carry data, since some only charge. Then check the setup guide in the settings.
- **No internet at the venue?** No problem. CHELFIE works fully offline, though Wi-Fi printers still need to be on the same network as your device.
- **On a tablet?** CHELFIE adjusts its layout to bigger screens.
- The screen stays on while the booth is running, so it won't go to sleep in the middle of a session.
